Safety & Quality Standards

Last updated: 22 May 2026

Food safety is non-negotiable at MeatPe. Every item that reaches your home goes through a strict quality and hygiene process designed for raw meat handling.

1. Sourcing

  • We work with licensed suppliers and abattoirs who meet local health authority standards.
  • Chicken and mutton are sourced fresh on the day of delivery — not stored from previous days.
  • Fish is sourced from regulated suppliers and inspected for freshness on arrival.

2. Processing & Hygiene

  • All cleaning, cutting, and packing happens in a dedicated, sanitised processing area.
  • Staff handling meat use gloves, aprons, headgear, and masks at all times.
  • Knives, boards, and surfaces are sanitised between batches.
  • Chicken, mutton, fish, and ready-to-cook items are processed in separate zones to prevent cross-contamination.

3. Cold Chain

  • From processing to your door, products stay in temperature-controlled storage and insulated delivery bags.
  • We do not use stale or repackaged inventory.

4. Packaging

  • Items are packed in food-grade, leak-proof containers.
  • Each pack is sealed and labelled with item name and weight.

5. If something is wrong

If you receive any item that is not fresh, mis-weighed, or damaged, message us on WhatsApp within 2 hours of delivery with a photo. We will replace it or refund the item — no questions asked.

6. Halal / Jhatka preference

Customers may specify Halal or Jhatka preference at the time of ordering. We honour the preference based on the supplier source for that day. If a specific preparation is unavailable on a given day, we will inform you before processing the order.

7. Customer responsibility

Once delivered, please refrigerate or cook immediately. Raw meat should not be left at room temperature for more than 2 hours. Wash hands, knives, and surfaces after handling.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information MeatPe collects, why we collect it, and how we handle it. We have written it in plain language. If anything is unclear, message us on WhatsApp or call +91 76175 55488.

1. What we collect

We collect only what is needed to deliver your order:

  • Name — to address you and label your order.
  • Phone number — to confirm orders, send delivery updates, and resolve issues.
  • Delivery address — to deliver to the correct location.
  • Order history — items you have purchased, dates, totals.
  • Optional notes — cleaning/cutting preferences, delivery instructions.
  • Basic device info — browser type, IP address, log data — used only for security and to fix bugs.

We do not collect aadhaar, PAN, bank account details, or full payment card numbers. Cash on delivery and UPI on delivery are the default payment methods at this stage.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To send order-related WhatsApp / SMS / phone updates.
  • To honour reward credits (MeatPe Cash) and Plus membership benefits.
  • To improve our service — for example, identifying which items go out of stock fastest.
  • To send occasional offers and updates. You may opt out anytime by replying STOP.

3. Who we share with

We share your information only with the people who help us complete your order:

  • Delivery partner — name, phone, and address (only the rider on your order).
  • Messaging providers (e.g. WhatsApp / SMS gateway) — phone number and message content needed to deliver the message.
  • Hosting providers — order data is stored on cloud servers we use to run MeatPe.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing.

4. How long we keep your information

  • Order records: kept for up to 3 years (for tax, accounting, and dispute purposes).
  • Account details: kept while your account is active; deleted on request.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you what information we have about you.
  • Correct any wrong information.
  • Delete your account and personal data (we will keep order records as required by law).
  • Stop sending you promotional messages.

To make any of these requests, message us on WhatsApp from your registered phone number, or call +91 76175 55488. We will respond within 15 working days.

6. Security

We use secure servers and encrypted connections (HTTPS) to protect your data. While no system is 100% secure, we follow industry standard practices to keep your information safe.

7. Children

MeatPe is intended for adults (18+). If you are a minor, please use the service under a parent or guardian's account.

8. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be communicated through the website or WhatsApp.
