🏢 About Us ▼

MeatPe is a fresh meat delivery service built for households who care about quality, hygiene, and convenience. We deliver freshly cut chicken, mutton, fish, and ready-to-cook items to your doorstep in 30 minutes — cleaned, cut, and packed exactly the way you want.

🛡️ Safety & Quality ▼

Last updated: 22 May 2026

Food safety is non-negotiable at MeatPe. Every item that reaches your home goes through a strict quality and hygiene process designed for raw meat handling.

1. Sourcing

  • We work with licensed suppliers and abattoirs who meet local health authority standards.
  • Chicken and mutton are sourced fresh on the day of delivery — not stored from previous days.
  • Fish is sourced from regulated suppliers and inspected for freshness on arrival.

2. Processing & Hygiene

  • All cleaning, cutting, and packing happens in a dedicated, sanitised processing area.
  • Staff handling meat use gloves, aprons, headgear, and masks at all times.
  • Knives, boards, and surfaces are sanitised between batches.
  • Chicken, mutton, fish, and ready-to-cook items are processed in separate zones to prevent cross-contamination.

3. Cold Chain

  • From processing to your door, products stay in temperature-controlled storage and insulated delivery bags.
  • We do not use stale or repackaged inventory.

4. Packaging

  • Items are packed in food-grade, leak-proof containers.
  • Each pack is sealed and labelled with item name and weight.

5. If something is wrong

If you receive any item that is not fresh, mis-weighed, or damaged, message us on WhatsApp within 2 hours of delivery with a photo. We will replace it or refund the item — no questions asked.

6. Halal / Jhatka preference

Customers may specify Halal or Jhatka preference at the time of ordering. We honour the preference based on the supplier source for that day. If a specific preparation is unavailable on a given day, we will inform you before processing the order.

7. Customer responsibility

Once delivered, please refrigerate or cook immediately. Raw meat should not be left at room temperature for more than 2 hours. Wash hands, knives, and surfaces after handling.
